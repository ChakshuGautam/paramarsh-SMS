## 🚀 Quick Login Process

1. **Navigate to**: `http://localhost:3001/sign-in`
2. **Select School**: "Swami Vivekanand Public School" from dropdown
3. **Select Branch**: Choose from:
   - Main Campus (full K-12 experience)
   - Senior Secondary Wing (Classes 11-12 focus)
   - Junior Wing (primary education focus)
4. **Enter Credentials**: Use any of the universal accounts above
5. **Click Sign In**: Access your branch-specific dashboard
